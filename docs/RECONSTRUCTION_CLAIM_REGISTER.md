# Reconstruction claim and source register

This register separates externally supported general principles from original derivations and authored synthetic scenarios. “Accessible” means the cited page or abstract/metadata was available for this reconstruction; it does not mean a source supports every nearby chapter statement. No full-text-access claim is made for papers whose full text was not inspected. No copied source text, figures, tables, or datasets are used.

## Evidence rule for future edits

Tie each externally sourced technical statement to the narrow source section that supports it. Treat reference presence as bibliography, not automatic evidence. Mark a result as calculation, local implementation check, simulation, empirical study, or industrial validation accurately. Only the first three are represented in the current candidate, and they do not establish site suitability. Do not imply source full-text review, copied-content clearance, equipment identification, or production qualification beyond what was actually performed.
